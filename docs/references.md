# References & Academic Citations

## Core Methodology References

### Linear Programming & Optimization

1. **Dantzig, G. B. (1963).** *Linear Programming and Extensions.* Princeton University Press.
   - Foundational text on LP theory and the simplex method
   - Relevant chapters: 1-4 (LP formulation and solution)

2. **Nocedal, J., & Wright, S. J. (2006).** *Numerical Optimization* (2nd ed.). Springer.
   - Chapter 10: Large-scale constrained optimization
   - Application to production mix problems

3. **Boyd, S., & Vandenberghe, L. (2004).** *Convex Optimization.* Cambridge University Press.
   - Chapters 4-5: Convex optimization problems
   - Covers LP as special case of convex optimization

### Vehicle Routing & Heuristics

4. **Laporte, G. (1992).** "The traveling salesman problem: An overview of algorithms." *European Journal of Operational Research*, 59(2), 231-247.
   - Comprehensive survey of TSP algorithms
   - Two-opt and local search methods

5. **Croes, G. A. (1958).** "A method for solving traveling salesman problems." *Operations Research*, 6(6), 791-812.
   - Original Two-opt algorithm paper
   - Foundational for route optimization

6. **Clarke, G., & Wright, W. (1964).** "Scheduling of vehicles from a central depot to a number of delivery points." *Operations Research*, 12(4), 568-581.
   - Clarke-Wright savings algorithm
   - Alternative heuristic for vehicle routing

### Lean Manufacturing & Theory of Constraints

7. **Goldratt, E. M. (1984).** *The Goal: A Process of Ongoing Improvement.* North River Press.
   - Foundational text on Theory of Constraints (TOC)
   - Bottleneck identification and management principles

8. **Goldratt, E. M., & Cox, J. (2004).** *The Goal* (3rd ed.). North River Press.
   - Updated edition with modern case studies
   - DBR (Drum-Buffer-Rope) scheduling

9. **Womack, J. P., & Jones, D. T. (2003).** *Lean Thinking: Banish Waste and Create Wealth in Your Corporation* (2nd ed.). Free Press.
   - Comprehensive overview of Lean manufacturing principles
   - Value stream mapping and waste elimination

10. **Shingo, S. (1989).** *A Study of the Toyota Production System.* Productivity Press.
    - Deep dive into Just-in-Time (JIT) and Kanban
    - Production leveling (Heijunka) principles

### Production Leveling (Heijunka)

11. **Monden, Y. (2012).** *Toyota Production System: An Integrated Approach to Just-in-Time* (4th ed.). CRC Press.
    - Chapter 3: Production leveling concepts
    - Mathematical models for Heijunka

12. **Bicheno, J. (2000).** *The Lean Toolbox.* PICSIE Books.
    - Practical Heijunka implementation guidance
    - Production smoothing techniques

### Supply Chain & Bullwhip Effect

13. **Lee, H. L., Padmanabhan, V., & Whang, S. (1997).** "The bullwhip effect in supply chains." *Sloan Management Review*, 38(3), 93-102.
    - Seminal paper on bullwhip effect
    - Root causes and mitigation strategies

14. **Christopher, M. (2016).** *Logistics & Supply Chain Management* (5th ed.). Pearson.
    - Chapter 9: Demand management and forecasting
    - Inventory buffering strategies

15. **Simchi-Levi, D., Kaminsky, P., & Simchi-Levi, E. (2003).** *Designing and Managing the Supply Chain: Concepts, Strategies and Case Studies* (2nd ed.). McGraw-Hill.
    - Comprehensive supply chain optimization
    - Production planning models
